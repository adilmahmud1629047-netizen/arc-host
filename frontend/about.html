How we work

Rules we hold ourselves to

Hosting pages are full of numbers nobody checks. These are the rules this site is built around.

Prices come from the API

Every price, billing cycle, and limit on the product pages is fetched from the catalog — if the API does not return it, the page does not show it.

No invented specifications

Hardware, regions, operating systems, and uptime figures stay unpublished until there is something real behind them.

One account for everything

Minecraft, web, and VPS plans sit in the same account and the same dashboard — no second login for a second product.

Honest contact details

This site lists no phone number or mailbox we do not actually monitor — the contact page explains how to reach us today.

Checkout when payments exist

Payment options are shown during checkout once payments are enabled — never advertised here as available before then.

Questions go to a person

Pre-sales and account questions go through the contact page and the dashboard — one route, tracked in one place.

At a glance

Small on purpose, specific where it counts

No founding year, no office photos, no customer counters. The numbers we do publish are the ones you can verify on the product pages.
